Sol Check — End-to-End System Architecture, Clinical Workflow & Diagram Suite

Complete operational architecture manual for autonomous crew health evaluation on Mars Sol 43 under 20–40 minute round-trip interplanetary communication latency.

Operational Mission Context: At an average orbital distance of 225 million kilometers from Earth, electromagnetic signals face a 20-minute one-way delay (40 minutes round-trip). In acute clinical anomalies, post-solar particle events, or insidious physiological deconditioning, astronauts cannot wait for Mission Control on Earth. Clinical intelligence must be autonomous, explainable, and air-gapped on-device.

8. Explainable Decision Rules Reference Table

Rule ID Rule Name Clinical Trigger Condition Operational Action
entry-quality Physiological Bounds Values outside survivable human limits or SBP ≤ DBP Rejects corrupted sensor data; orders recalibration
combined-sleep-mood Behavioral Cascade Sleep < 6.0h AND Mood ≤ 2 across ≥ 3 consecutive sols Escalates to CMO; orders sleep hygiene & duty rest
sustained-trend Longitudinal Drift ≥ 3 consecutive readings shifting monotonically Flags insidious deconditioning (e.g. fluid shifts)
baseline-change Acute Anomaly Single reading deviates by ≥ metric demo delta Highlights 3D Twin hotspot; emits clinical warning
reported-symptoms Symptom Recurrence Active reported symptoms (e.g. cephalalgia, fatigue) Correlates symptoms with active mission hazards
archive-replay-context Radiation Event Nexus Correlated with Sol 41 Curiosity RAD storm (>15 μGy/h) Contextualizes crew fatigue with habitat storm shelter
data-unavailable Evidence Disclosure Fewer than 5 distinct measured sols recorded Discloses uncertainty; prevents premature triage
